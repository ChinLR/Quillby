Conditions of Carriage

Version 4.2, effective 1 January 2026

Section 9: Delays, cancellations and compensation

9.1 Care during a delay

If your flight is delayed by two hours or more, we will give you meals and refreshments in reasonable relation to the waiting time.

9.2 Compensation for delay

If your flight arrives at its final destination more than three hours after its scheduled arrival time, you may be entitled to compensation under UK261. The amount depends on the distance flown: £220 for flights up to 1,500 km and £350 for flights between 1,500 km and 3,500 km.

9.3 Extraordinary circumstances

We do not pay compensation where the delay was caused by extraordinary circumstances that could not have been avoided, for example severe weather or air traffic control strikes.

9.4 Technical and operational causes

Technical faults found during routine maintenance and delays caused by our own scheduling or crew rostering are not extraordinary circumstances. A delay for operational reasons does not by itself remove your right to compensation.

9.5 How compensation is paid

Compensation is paid in cash or by bank transfer. We may offer travel vouchers only if you agree in writing.

9.6 Making a claim

You can claim directly with us. You do not need a solicitor or a claims company.

Section 10: Baggage

10.1 Checked baggage

Checked baggage must weigh no more than 23 kg per bag unless you have paid for extra allowance.
